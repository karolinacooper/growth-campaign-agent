# Google Ads plan — D1 US purchase terms (planned)

**Goal:** appear on every ICP purchase search in the US, even at ~10 searches/month. Coverage, not reach.
**Budget:** ~PLN 2,500/month to start. **Conversions:** GA4 booking, form, email click (imported).

## Structure
| Campaign | Ad group | Keywords (phrase + exact) | Landing page |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1_US_Purchase | Medical device software | medical device software development services · medical device software development company · medical device software development companies · software development for medical devices | service page titled with the main term |
| D1_US_Purchase | Healthcare AI consulting | healthcare ai consulting | healthcare AI consulting page |
| D1_US_Purchase | MLOps | mlops consulting services · mlops consulting | MLOps for FDA page |
| D1_US_Purchase | IEC 62304 | iec 62304 medical device software | IEC 62304 guide (with CTA) |
| D1_US_Adjacent (lower bids, test) | HIPAA and cloud | hipaa compliant ai · hipaa compliant aws · hipaa compliant cloud | HIPAA AI architecture page |
| D1_US_Adjacent | Data platform | healthcare data platform · healthcare data engineering | data platforms page |
| D1_US_Adjacent | Imaging data | medical image annotation · medical data annotation | annotation workflow page |

Not bought (Low search volume): samd development company/services, healthcare mlops company, fda software consultant, aws healthcare consulting partner, healthcare data engineering company/consultant, medical ai development services.

## Settings
- Search only (no Display / partners); location US, "presence"
- Bidding: Maximise conversions until ~30 conversions/30 days, then tCPA
- Shared negatives: jobs, careers, salary, course, certification, free, template, pdf, tutorial, intern, wiki, plus irrelevant devices/industries found in search terms
- RSAs: 2 per ad group, built from the messaging angles (FDA-ready, notebook to production, HIPAA/security, healthcare-only)
- Assets: sitelinks (case studies, how we work, contact/calendar), callouts, structured snippets

## Watch-outs
- Very low volumes: judge by search-term quality and booked meetings, not CTR
- "hipaa compliant ai" (4,400/mo, PLN 40–195 CPC) can eat the budget: cap the Adjacent campaign at ≤ 30% of spend
