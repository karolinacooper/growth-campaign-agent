# Brand voice and claims rules — Example Company

- **Positioning line:** "the data and AI team for healthcare and medtech sectors" (as the CEO says it in the videos)
- **Tone:** expert, direct, calm. Speaks like an architect who has been "on the other side of the table" with the FDA. Not casual: avoid "stuck on", "grab", throwaway jokes.
- **Voice:** first person as the CEO for Thought Leader / video ads; company voice for Page ads.
- **Always:** say "AI model" (never just "model") so it can't read as business model; name the problem before the offer; one clear CTA.
- **Avoid:** em dashes; hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"); vague claims; mixed CTAs (e.g. "find me at the booth" + "book a slot" in the same breath).
- **Approved proof points:** healthcare-only focus; engineers who specialise in healthcare and physicians on the team; CEO runs the first call and stays on as architect. Client names and numbers only once the client approves them.
- **Event copy (HLTH):** booth is secondary; the ask is "book a slot". Slot length must match the booking page (20 vs 30 minutes).
- **LinkedIn limits:** intro text ≤ 600 characters (hook in the first ~150), headline ≤ 70 (aim ≤ 55).
- **CTA button:** Register (no "Book" option on LinkedIn); Learn more for "see our work first" ads.
- **Spelling:** US English for US audience.
