# Skill 01 — Market analysis

Produce the market analysis that every campaign decision rests on.

## Inputs
`config/client.md`, the client website, any client docs, web search, Google Keyword Planner, LinkedIn audience estimates, Semrush/Ahrefs if available.

## Output
`analysis/market-analysis.md` using `templates/market-analysis.md`.

## Steps
1. **Product & positioning.** Read the site (home, pricing, product, case studies). Summarise the product, value proposition, pricing model and proof points. Flag anything unclear as a question for the client.
2. **ICP & buying committee.** Define firmographics, roles, pains, triggers and objections. Map each role to the channel where we can reach it (LinkedIn job titles, search intent).
3. **Competitors (5–8).** For each: positioning, pricing, the keywords they bid on, their ads (Google Ads Transparency Center, LinkedIn Ad Library, Meta Ad Library), and the gaps we can own.
4. **Demand & intent.**
   - Search: keyword clusters (problem / solution / category / competitor / brand) with volume, CPC and intent tier, per market.
   - LinkedIn: audience size for the ICP per market, with expected CPC/CPM ranges.
5. **Messaging angles.** 3–5 angles, each tied to a pain and a proof point. These seed `skills/08-creative-testing.md`.
6. **Channel plan & budget.** Recommend channels, budget split, and expected CPL / SQL range, with assumptions stated. Include a "what would make us change this plan" line.
7. **Risks & open questions** for the client.
8. Save, then ask for approval before stage 3.

## Quality bar
- Every number has a source and a date.
- Keyword volumes are per market, not global.
- Recommendations are falsifiable: they say what result in 30 days would prove them wrong.
