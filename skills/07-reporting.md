# Skill 07 — Reporting

## Weekly (client-facing, short)
`reports/YYYY-Www.md` from `templates/weekly-report.md`:
- Headline: pipeline € and SQLs this week vs. target and last week
- Spend, leads, CPL, cost per SQL by channel
- What we changed and why (from the change log)
- What we learned (from learnings)
- Next week's plan

## Monthly (review meeting)
`reports/YYYY-MM-monthly.md`:
- Full funnel by channel: spend → leads → MQL → SQL → opportunities → pipeline € → closed-won
- Pipeline-to-spend ratio and CAC trend
- Top / bottom campaigns, keywords, audiences, creatives
- Tests run and results
- Budget recommendation for next month, with reasoning
- Risks and asks for the client

## Rules
- Lead with business outcomes (pipeline, SQLs), then channel metrics.
- Every number traceable to a source export, stored alongside the report or linked.
- Plain language. One chart per key point, max.
- Be honest about bad weeks: say what happened and what we're doing about it.
